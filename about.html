About

Curious about how things work, all the way down.

I like the whole stack: the screen someone taps, the API behind it, and the network packets in between. Here's how I got here and what I work with.

I studied business in secondary school, then found programming and never looked back. At Reboot Coding Institute I learned by building: no lectures, just projects reviewed by peers. That's where I wrote real-time servers in Go, containerised apps with Docker, and later picked up Rust for games and graphics.

Alongside Reboot I'm completing an ICT Programming degree at Bahrain Polytechnic, where my coursework leans into security: cryptography, digital forensics, risk management and intrusion detection.

I've worked on two products used by real customers: a laundry service marketplace at Exceeders, and the Esbitar clinic platform, where I built across mobile, web and backend as a full-stack intern.

Outside work I'm into social and party games, the kind that fill a living room at a family gathering. That's what pulled me into building Baytna and Seen Jeem.
